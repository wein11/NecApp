## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output, native i18n) + GSAP for motion. Vercel as review environment, DonWeb shared hosting as production (static files uploaded by FTP from GitHub Actions). Decided with the user in the discovery interview (see docs/brief-landing.md).

## Users

Four audiences, all targeted for lead capture across Latin America:

1. Hospitals and rehabilitation centres (ENT, neurology, audiology, physiotherapy services; purchase signed by service heads or procurement).
2. Universities and research laboratories (biomechanics, gait labs).
3. Sports clinics and high-performance centres.
4. Independent professionals (physiotherapists, audiologists, ENT doctors in private practice).

Their job on the site: understand what each software does, trust that NecApp can deliver and support it locally, and ask for a demo, a quote or a brochure.

## Product Purpose

NecApp is the official distributor, with license to sell, of medical software in Latin America. Today: Vicon Nexus and Vicon Polygon (motion capture and clinical gait reporting) and NeuroEars Anna and NeuroEars Thera (VR-based vestibular assessment and vestibular rehabilitation). More software will be added over time, so the brand message must not be tied to the current catalogue. Success: qualified leads (demo requests, quote requests, brochure downloads, WhatsApp contacts).

## Positioning

Turnkey local partner: NecApp delivers the system working, with installation, training and support in the client's language, instead of the client buying directly from a manufacturer in the UK or Korea. NecApp is a company of Necod Argentina SRL.

## Operating Context

- Anna: Class II software as medical device for vestibular function tests. Records nystagmus with a head-mounted display with eye-tracking cameras; baseline, positional, oculomotor, SVV/SVH and caloric tests. Used by trained ENT, neurologists, audiologists. Assists diagnosis; does not diagnose on its own.
- Thera: personalised vestibular rehabilitation with HMD; gaze stability exercises verified by eye tracking; DHI, VADL and V-Screen 9 questionnaires scored with AI; exportable reports. Adjunct tool, not intended to diagnose.
- Nexus: Vicon's core life-sciences application: captures, calibrates and processes 3D motion data, syncs force plates, EMG and video; Plug-in Gait and CGM2 models.
- Polygon: produces clinical gait reports from Nexus data.
- Narrative: Anna assesses, Thera rehabilitates (full vestibular patient circuit); Vicon measures movement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Languages: neutral Spanish (default), English, Portuguese. Adapt, do not translate literally.
- All products hold ANMAT registration (numbers pending from client).
- Regulatory language: NeuroEars products "assist the professional"; never claim they diagnose, cure or treat.
- Undecided: whether hardware is sold, licence model, demo modality (presential or remote). Do not state these.
- Forms post by email through Web3Forms or Formspree (no backend). Analytics: GA4 with events for form submissions and WhatsApp clicks; Meta and LinkedIn pixels prepared but off.

## Product Principles

1. Clinical credibility first: every claim must be true and regulator-safe.
2. Local and human: the reason to buy from NecApp is the people who install, train and answer in the client's language.
3. Catalogue-agnostic brand: new software must slot in without redesign.
4. Every visit can become a lead, with the lightest possible ask.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Audience includes older clinicians; generous text sizes and contrast. Reduced-motion support for all animation.
