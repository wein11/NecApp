# Surface brief: landing (home + product pages)

Mode: Persuade.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The pulse inside the "A" of the NecApp logo becomes the site's signal. Half the screen is a fixed navy monitor where one teal trace turns into what each product measures: heartbeat for the brand, knee flexion over the gait cycle for Vicon, nystagmus slow and fast phases for Anna, a gaze-fixation target for Thera. Refuses the category default of hero + logo strip + equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper ground `#F3F6F8`, navy ink `#032A5F` for text and the monitor field, teal `#039E8A` reserved for the trace and the primary action, deep teal `#00705F` for text links. Display Lexend (brand-like geometric), text Literata (clinical-journal serif). Square corners, flat fields, no rules, no shadows at rest.

STORY: A clinician or lab head sees what NecApp brings (world-class medical software installed and supported locally), recognises their own measurement on the monitor, and asks for a demo, a quote, a brochure or writes on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 45/55 split. Left paper column: logo lockup with tagline, language switch, h1 "Software médico de clase mundial", one sentence, "Solicitar demo" solid teal plus "Ver soluciones" text link. Right: sticky full-height navy monitor drawing the pulse with live readout. Phone: monitor 56svh on top, column slides over it.

FORM: Split stage (taste-skill split-stage, seed 1305817764) fused with impeccable grounded candidate 7 (instrument trace). seed:89091dbfb260cffa (roll degraded, no challengers).

Signature interaction: drag or hover along the trace to read real values (gait cycle %, knee angle; nystagmus slow-phase velocity); tabs switch the trace. Motion grammar: traces draw with stroke-dashoffset, section parts rise in a 70ms stagger, reduced motion shows the static final trace.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
